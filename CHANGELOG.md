## 1.0.0

First release on pub.dev.

- Theme: `Win98Theme` / `Win98ThemeData` with a full `copyWith` and value
  equality, and the bundled Pixelify Sans pixel font.
- Building blocks: `Bevel` (raised, window, pressed, field, status,
  thin-raised, etched, outline), `PixelGlyph` / `Win98Glyphs`, `FocusRect`
  and `Win98PopupAnchor`.
- Windows: `Win98Window` (title bar, menu bar, toolbar, status bar),
  `Win98Desktop`, `Win98Taskbar` with a Start menu, dialogs, message boxes
  and the color dialog.
- Menus: `Win98MenuBar` with cascading submenus and keyboard navigation,
  `Win98ContextMenu` on right-click or long press, and `Win98MenuPanel`.
- Controls: buttons, tool buttons, checkboxes, radios, slider, drop-down,
  text fields (password, read-only, disabled, multi-line), tooltips, tabs,
  group boxes, progress and status bars.
- Lists: `Win98ListBox`, `Win98ListView` (columns with sortable headers),
  `Win98TreeView` and `Win98ScrollView` / `Win98Scrollbar`.
